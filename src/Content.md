---

# Our Vision

## Making the World Pleasing to God

Our vision is to **make the world pleasing to God through the Word of Faith, Holiness, and the power of the Holy Spirit**.

We believe that a transformed world begins with transformed people.

When men and women encounter Jesus Christ, understand God's Word, submit themselves to the work of the Holy Spirit, and choose to walk in righteousness, they become instruments of God's purpose in their families, workplaces, communities, and nations.

Our desire is therefore to raise believers who know God, love His Word, walk in holiness, operate in faith, and demonstrate the character of Christ.

---

# Our Mission

Faith & Fire Ministries exists to:

* Proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

* Reach our local community and extend the Gospel beyond Johannesburg.

---

# Our Core Foundations

## 1. Undiluted Holiness

We believe that holiness is an essential part of the Christian walk.

The grace of God does not lead us away from righteousness; it empowers us to live lives that honour Him.

We therefore encourage believers to pursue purity, integrity, obedience, righteousness, and a lifestyle that reflects the nature of Christ.

Our emphasis on holiness is rooted in our desire to see Christians become genuine representatives of Jesus wherever they go.

---

## 2. The Word of Faith

Faith begins with hearing and believing the Word of God.

We believe the Scriptures provide the foundation for understanding God, His promises, His will, and His purpose for our lives.

Through preaching, teaching, discipleship, and personal study of Scripture, we encourage believers to develop a faith that is rooted in God's Word rather than circumstances.

Our goal is to help people move from simply hearing the Word to **believing it, living it, and applying it**.

---

## 3. The Power of the Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is actively involved in the life of the believer and the Church.

The Christian life cannot be lived effectively through human strength alone.

We therefore welcome the ministry of the Holy Spirit and encourage believers to develop a deeper relationship with God through prayer, worship, Scripture, and spiritual fellowship.

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers for holy living, effective witness, spiritual growth, service, and the fulfilment of God's purpose.
